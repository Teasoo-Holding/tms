# 2027 technology budget

`TMS-2027-Technology-Budget.xlsx` (issue #55) is the 2027 operating budget for TMS.

- **Summary**: the 2027 total in USD and NGN, split by service, with dependencies and uncertainties.
- **Assumptions**: every input. Blue cells can be changed; yellow cells are estimates to confirm. The other sheets recalculate from them.
- **Monthly**: data volumes and costs month by month.
- **Line items**: each service, its plan, billing and notes. Enter quotes in the yellow one-time cells.

Prices were checked on 9 Nov 2026 (Neon, Cloudflare R2, Render, Vercel pricing pages). Check the providers' usage pages each month and update Assumptions.

Teasoo Tree Management System (TMS)

Summary of capabilities and a test script · Teasoo Consulting · October 2026

1. What the system is

TMS records every tree planted on Teasoo's reforestation projects and every later visit to it, offline in the field, with no data loss. It has three parts:

  • Android app: used by field officers and administrators. Works fully offline after the first sign-in and sends data when the phone has internet.
  • Web portal (tms.teasooconsulting.com): used for administration, dashboards, reports and exports. Clients can view their project through a secret share link.
  • Cloud server and database: stores records and photos, enforces who can do what, and keeps an audit log of every change.

Trees have no individual GPS. Each planting year's land survey divides the area into sectors and sub-sectors, recorded by their corner points, and a tree is located by its sector and sub-sector.

3. Capabilities

Field work (Android)

  • Simple home: one large "Start field session" button. During a session, one large "Register trees" button and a red "End session" button. Large text and buttons.
  • Field session: choose project, planting year, vendor (if the year has vendors), sector and sub-sector once. Change sub-sector from a drop-down as you move.
  • Register trees: type only the tree number. The full ID is built from the project's format; numbers run on per project and year, shared by all vendors, and the next one is suggested. Species and status are remembered. A photo is required, shown on the form with Retake and Remove. After saving, a confirmation shows the tree's ID before the next form. Date, time and planting date are recorded automatically.
  • Known IDs: if a tree number is already registered, the app shows the tree's photo, last species and status, and offers "Record a visit".
  • Find a tree: search by tree number (with photos), open the tree and record a visit: Alive or Dead, a photo, a note and custom fields. The date and time are stamped automatically.
  • Warnings: the app warns if a tree is visited in a different sector than the one it was registered in.
  • Tree page: the latest photo at the top, all photos, full visit history and who recorded each visit. Corrections are kept as amendments; nothing is overwritten.
  • Replanting: plant a replacement for a dead tree. It gets a new ID linked to the old tree; admins can type the ID by hand.
  • My visits: a list of days with times and counts; open a day to see each tree registered or visited, with its photo.

Offline, sync and safety

  • Saving: every record is saved on the phone first and sent automatically when there is internet. Sending twice never creates duplicates.
  • Duplicate IDs entered on two phones are kept as conflicts for an admin to resolve; neither is overwritten.
  • Ready to send: shows records and photos waiting, with Send now.
  • Sign-out is blocked while anything is unsent.
  • Photos: resized to about 300 KB before saving. The phone deletes its copy only after the server confirms it has the photo, and normally keeps a copy for 30 days. Free up space removes confirmed copies early.
  • Light backup: Download/TMS/TMS-backup.zip is kept up to date and survives clearing the app or reinstalling it. Export backup includes photos; Restore puts records back in the queue.
  • Data clear: when an admin clears the server data, each phone saves a backup, then resets.

Administration (web and Android)

  • Projects: each has planting years. Every year has its own surveyed sectors and sub-sectors, entered from corner points (walk to a corner and tap "Use my location").
  • Vendors per planting year.
  • Tree ID format: one per project, shared by all vendors: fixed parts (project, year, fixed text) and changing parts (sector, sub-sector, tree number). Tree numbers run on per project and planting year. Changes affect new trees only.
  • Custom fields: extra fields such as a nursery batch number, collected at registration or at every visit. They can be remembered for the rest of the day.
  • Reference lists: species, statuses and vendors. Anything can be edited, deactivated or deleted (long-press or swipe on the phone, ⋯ on the web). Items still in use are protected.
  • Users: sign in with a username, phone number or email. Admins can reset passwords and assign people to projects or areas. Each user's activity and last sync are shown.
  • Conflicts and corrections: resolve duplicate IDs and correct serial numbers. Every change is audited.
  • Activity log: the latest 10 entries, or the full log filtered by date, person or activity.
  • Clear data: field data only, or everything. Needs the typed confirmation and your password.

Dashboards, reports and sharing

  • Dashboard: trees planted, alive and dead, not yet visited, status by sector and species, alive/dead map by area, and weekly field activity.
  • Filters: project, planting year, vendor, sector, sub-sector, species, status and date range.
  • Visits table: every visit, newest first, with CSV export; open a row to see its photos.
  • Density map: each sector and sub-sector drawn from its corner points and shaded by trees per hectare, with alive share and a table (web, Android Administration, and the client link). Trees have no GPS of their own, so density is shown per area.
  • Photos everywhere: wherever a tree is shown, its photos are too; the latest is shown large on the tree page.
  • Exports: trees as CSV or Excel. Export all gives one Excel workbook containing every trees, visits and configuration sheet. Print report saves the dashboard as a PDF.
  • Share with client: creates a secret link for one project, with an optional expiry, which can be revoked at any time. The client sees a read-only dashboard with Excel, CSV and PDF downloads. Staff names are hidden and each use is recorded.
  • Photo loading: lists load small thumbnails (about 20 KB); the full photo loads only when opened.

4. Test script

Before you start: an administrator account and a field officer account (ask Teasoo's administrator). The latest TMS Field app installed on an Android phone (8.0 or newer). A computer browser for the web portal. Tick each step when the expected result is seen; note anything different in the margin.
Test on a test project. Section F clears data: run it only on a system where that is intended.
